Event Highlights

Five massive festive attractions curated for an electric Onam experience.

Chendamelam

High-energy traditional Kerala percussion ensemble unleashing thrilling acoustic rhythms.

Vadam Vali

Electrifying Tug-of-War arena battle testing strength, stamina, and team spirit.

Uri Adi

Traditional blindfolded pot breaking challenge with roaring festive cheer.

Dance Program

High-energy live group dance performances to popular cinematic songs right before Sadhya!

Onam Sadhya

Grand 100% Pure Veg traditional feast served on fresh banana leaves (included with entry pass).

DJ Evening

High-voltage sunset DJ session blending South Indian festival tracks & party hits.

Important Guidelines

Rules & Entry Policies

To ensure a safe and memorable celebration for all attendees, strict adherence is enforced.

Strictly Stag Entry Only

THAKRUTHA 2026 is strictly designed for stag ticket holders. Government ID verification will be conducted at the venue check-in desk.

Strict Zero Tolerance Policy

No Drugs and No Alcohol allowed. Security search will be conducted at entry. Any violation results in immediate pass cancellation without refund.
